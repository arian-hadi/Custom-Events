How It Works

What is Editor Ranking?

The Editor Ranking is a competitive leaderboard system that ranks video editors based on their performance, community engagement, and consistent participation. Editors compete across platforms (YouTube and TikTok) to climb the rankings and showcase their editing skills.

How it works: Your ranking is determined by your total points, which are calculated from your video's platform performance metrics, community upvotes, and participation consistency. The higher your points, the higher your rank on the leaderboard.

What you need to do: To participate, you must first apply to become an editor and get accepted. Once accepted, you can submit your edits for competitions like "Edit of the Week" to earn points and climb the rankings. Consistency and quality content are key to success!

How Points Are Calculated

Your total points are calculated from three main components: Platform Performance, Community Upvotes, and Participation Bonus.

1. Platform Performance Points

Based on your video's performance metrics, normalized fairly for creators of all sizes.

YouTube Formula

  • • Normalized Views × 35%
  • • Normalized Likes × 25%
  • • Normalized Comments × 20%
  • • Engagement Rate × 20%

TikTok Formula

  • • Normalized Views × 30%
  • • Normalized Likes × 35%
  • • Normalized Comments × 15%
  • • Engagement Rate × 20%

Fairness Features:

  • ✓ Logarithmic Scaling: Smaller creators compete fairly with larger ones
  • ✓ Engagement Multipliers: High engagement rates get bonuses (up to 50% bonus for >20% engagement)
  • ✓ Small Creator Bonus: Creators with <1K followers and >5% engagement get +5 points
  • ✓ Capped Normalization: Follower/subscriber count capped at 1M for fair comparison

2. Community Upvote Points

2 points per upvote, with a maximum of 50 points (25 upvotes).

Each user can upvote up to 3 edits per week. Upvotes show community appreciation for your work!

3. Participation Bonus

15 points per consecutive week of participation (starting from week 2).

Example: Week 1 = 0 bonus, Week 2 = 15 points, Week 3 = 30 points, Week 4 = 45 points, etc.

Total Points Formula

Total = Platform Points + Upvote Points (max 50) + Participation Bonus

Tips to Maximize Your Points

  • • Focus on high engagement rates (likes + comments relative to views) for bonus multipliers
  • • Submit consistently each week to build your participation bonus
  • • Create quality content that encourages upvotes from the community
  • • Smaller creators can compete effectively thanks to logarithmic scaling and engagement bonuses

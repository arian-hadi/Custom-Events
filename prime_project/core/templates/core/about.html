About 2.0Transformers

2.0Transformers is a creative community platform dedicated to showcasing and promoting video editors' work. We feature editor rankings, competitions, and a curated collection of editing content from YouTube and TikTok creators. All content is displayed with proper attribution and consent from creators.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create a vibrant community where video editors can showcase their talent, compete in friendly competitions, and gain recognition for their creative work. We believe in fostering creativity, supporting emerging editors, and celebrating the art of video editing.

What We Offer

  • Editor Rankings: A comprehensive leaderboard system that ranks editors based on their performance, community engagement, and consistent participation.
  • Edit Competitions: Weekly and monthly competitions like "Edit of the Week" where editors can submit their best work and compete for recognition.
  • Community Features: Browse and discover amazing edits from talented creators across YouTube and TikTok platforms.
  • Shop: Discover 2.0Transformers editing products and carefully selected affiliated merchandise.

Legal Compliance & Data Privacy

We take legal compliance and data privacy seriously. All video content displayed on our platform is embedded using official platform APIs (TikTok, YouTube) with proper attribution. Content creators retain full ownership and rights to their work.

User data is collected with explicit consent and used solely for content display, rankings, and promotional purposes. We do not access, store, or share private or sensitive information from connected platforms. For more details, please see our Privacy Policy and Disclaimers.
